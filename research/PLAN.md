# Research plan

Author: Ahmed Elmi
Status: planned

## First block: 30 research hours
1. Understand the task lifecycle and distinguish saved state from current authorization.
2. Inspect the original Hervat lab when available; establish the actual starting point.
3. Predict and test behavior in a local simulation with synthetic data.
4. Compare interrupted and uninterrupted tasks, including legitimate completion.
5. Implement a learner-authored control and test it against the same scenarios.
6. Publish reproducible evidence, limitations and open questions.

Time is a planning budget, not a completion claim. No assertion of novelty until relevant literature and existing implementations have been compared.

## Evidence required for a result
Research question; threat model; hypothesis recorded before the run; exact commit; environment and dependencies; commands; synthetic inputs; raw outputs; control results; repetitions where nondeterminism matters; interpretation; limitations; reviewer notes; AI-assistance disclosure.
